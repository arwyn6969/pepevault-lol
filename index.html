PepeVault is the traveling exhibition for the real on-chain Pepe lore. We start at the source: RARE PEPES — 1,774 cards across 36 series on Counterparty, the 2016 movement that quietly invented on-chain art before anyone called it that.

From there, the lineage continues. FAKERARES. NOTABLE PEPES. BITCOIN STAMPS. The full subculture the community has carried since the first card dropped.

No AI Pepes. No fabricated assets. We show up with the real frogs and the real artists, and we run the show like an underground festival — raw industrial spaces, painted flyers, loud community, no white cubes.

Three nights locked for 2026 — Lisbon by day, Stampfest after dark, Prague to close it out. After that, anywhere there's a wall to paste a poster on.
