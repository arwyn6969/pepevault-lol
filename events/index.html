01 / THE RESIDENCY

PEPEVAULT × NFC LISBON

JUNE 4 – 6 · 2026

Three days. One vault. The Counterparty originals leave the cold storage and travel to the Atlantic.

PepeVault sets up inside NFC Lisbon for the conference's full run — a third-floor gallery integrated with the old machinery of a historic flour mill, with an outdoor Agora container running cyber-lounge programming alongside. A walk-through exhibition of the 2016 Rare Pepes, the FAKERARES, the Notable Pepes, and a corner reserved for Bitcoin Stamps. Thirty-one artists across the wall. A Featured Exhibition Partner pod — Kaleidoscope XCP, curated by Nathan Sonic — anchoring the back room. Cross-curations with the other NFC exhibits for maximum chaos.

No NFTs. No screens pretending to be paintings. The cards as they were minted, framed and lit, with the people who made them around to talk.

Entry is free with your NFC Lisbon ticket. Bring your wallet. Stay for the late night.

02 / THE FLOCK PARTY

STAMPFEST 3 — LISBON

JUNE · LATE NIGHT

The stamps come out at night.

Stampfest returns to Lisbon for its third edition — The Flock Party, sponsored by Stampchain.io to celebrate the third anniversary of Bitcoin Stamps. Co-presented with PepeVault and built inside the same square mile as the NFC residency. The room is small on purpose. The address moves on purpose. If you're around, you'll hear about it.

An audiovisual immersive — visuals from the PepeVault artists mixed live by DITA, music from FAKE DJ PEPE, ARWYN, and an OG special guest to be announced. Bitcoin Stamps on the walls. Counterparty in the conversation. The crew that has been carrying the protocol since the original drops, in one room, off the schedule.

If you came to NFC Lisbon for the panels, this is the part of the trip you'll tell people about.

03 / THE WARMUP

PEPEX PRAGUE

JUNE 11 · 2026

Prague. BTC Week. The bar upstairs.

PepeVault crosses the continent for PEPEX Prague — a one-night vault drop at Fuchs2 on Štvanice Island, an industrial venue on the river. PepeVault takes the bar upstairs as the warmup floor for the 600000000000.com party downstairs, during BTC Prague week. AV setup, full livestream from pepevault.lol/live. The Týn Church above ground, the Pepes below.

The cards travel with the show: Rare Pepes, FAKERARES, Notable Pepes, Stamps. The same vault that opens in Lisbon opens here, one week later, in a different city, in a different mood. Prague has its own gravity.

Free with your host ticket. Show up early — the room fills fast.
